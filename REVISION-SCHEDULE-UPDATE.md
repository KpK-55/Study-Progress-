# Revision Schedule update

- Review intervals now follow Option A: Day 3, Day 7, Day 15 and Day 30.
- The dashboard's Chapter revision due panel links to a dedicated Revision Schedule view.
- The new view shows upcoming review cards and a date-wise schedule with All, Today, Pending and Completed filters.
- Marking a review as reviewed advances it to the next interval; after Day 30, the cycle repeats every 30 days.
- Existing schedule records created under the previous interval cycle are not automatically migrated. New completions use the new cycle. Existing records retain their currently stored due date and interval index.
